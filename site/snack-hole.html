Snack Hole

Tiny hole. Big appetite.

Guide a very hungry donut through small snack worlds. Scoop, grow and collect treasures in a gentle arcade game with no rush and plenty of little wins.

Play the game ↗Browse the source ↗

Inside the experience

  • Finger, mouse and keyboard controls move the donut through a Canvas 2D world.
  • Procedural snacks, sound effects and celebrations are created in the browser.
  • Unlockable stickers and donut styles are saved locally.
  • Deterministic round simulations check reachability, resizing and completion.

Built with purpose

A small game for dexterity and delight. All play happens in the browser, without paid services.

See the game README for setup, checks and limitations.
