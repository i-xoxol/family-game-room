Reading Garden

A sound becomes a word. A word opens a story.

Eric Reads starts with visible letters and isolated sounds, then moves through blending, building a heard word and finding its spelling. Six paths and three original read-along tales keep practice small and purposeful.

Play the game ↗Browse the source ↗

Inside the experience

  • Tap visible graphemes to hear their sounds. Drag from the first sound to the last to blend.
  • A large “Hear the word” button replays the spelling prompt; the word also plays when building begins.
  • Sound boxes, spoken mouth-position hints, short sentences and three illustrated stories connect listening with print.
  • 266 prepared clips play locally. No microphone, model or synthesis request is used during play.

Built with purpose

The teaching approach reinforces foundational US English decoding. Original materials draw on public district guidance and the IES practice guide; the app is not an official curriculum or a measured reading intervention.
